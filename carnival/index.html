📜 How to Play

Goal: pick a Performer, build a deck of cards as you travel the map, and defeat the Ringmaster at the end of Act 4. Losing all HP ends the run.
Combat: each turn you draw a hand and spend Energy playing cards — Attacks deal damage, Skills grant Block/utility, Powers apply a lasting effect once. Enemies telegraph their next move above their head.
Momentum: every card has a Sigil (🎪 Ring, 🎭 Mask, 🕯️ Candle, or 🃏 Wild). Playing 3 matching Sigils in a row this turn triggers a free Encore effect for that Sigil — Wild cards always continue a streak.
Between fights: shops sell cards/relics/card removal, rest sites heal or upgrade a card, events offer risky choices, and treasure chests hold relics — passive artifacts that reshape your whole run.
Meta: every run (win or lose) earns Tickets, spent permanently in the Backstage to unlock new Performers and cards. Ascension levels add modifiers for a tougher, more rewarding run.
Bestiary: defeating the same enemy type 50 times (lifetime, across all runs) permanently teaches you its weakness — +15% damage dealt to it — and 100 kills teaches a deeper weakness, starting every future fight against it with Vulnerable already applied. Check the Bestiary from the Big Top to track your progress.
